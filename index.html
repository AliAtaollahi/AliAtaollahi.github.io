01 About

My work sits where formal verification, programming languages, and dependable systems meet.

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran, where I began exploring how precise models can help us reason about software, hardware, and cyber-physical systems.

I enjoy research that connects strong theory with working tools: finding the right abstraction, shaping an algorithm around it, and testing the result on systems that matter.
